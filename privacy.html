Privacy Policy

Precision Cellular Immunology (PCI) respects your privacy and handles account, order, and inquiry data in accordance with U.S. federal and Texas state law.

Full policy under review with counsel

Our formal Privacy Policy is currently being finalized. For a copy of the current draft, or to make a data-access, data-portability, or deletion request under applicable law, contact us and we'll respond within one business day.

What we collect

  • Account data — email address, name, phone, and shipping address you provide when signing up or checking out.
  • Order data — items you purchase and the status of your orders.
  • Analytics — anonymous page-view and performance data via Vercel Web Analytics (no cookies, no cross-site tracking).
  • Payment data — handled directly by Stripe. Card details never touch PCI servers.

Who we share with

Supabase (account + order storage), Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting + analytics), and Roboflow (AI model hosting for the Slide Viewer). Nothing else. We do not sell personal data.
